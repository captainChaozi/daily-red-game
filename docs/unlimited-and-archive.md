# Unlimited practice and the challenge archive

[Daily Red Pokemon](https://daily-red.app/) changes your six allies every day at 00:00 UTC. Red's team never changes. Two pages help you practise outside the one official daily battle.

## Unlimited

[daily-red.app/unlimited](https://daily-red.app/unlimited) loads the current date's team for repeatable 6v6 battles (up to 120 turns). The optional tactics are the same as the daily: one tactical swap, a bonus goal, and two Scouts.

## Archive of past teams

Each day has its own challenge page, for example:

- [2026-10-28](https://daily-red.app/challenges/2026-10-28)
- [2026-10-27](https://daily-red.app/challenges/2026-10-27)
- [2026-10-26](https://daily-red.app/challenges/2026-10-26)

Older teams are a good way to test whether a plan against Red's fixed six (Pikachu leads; then Charizard, Blastoise, Venusaur, Snorlax, Espeon) generalises.

## Suggested routine

1. Read today's matchups box.
2. Run two Unlimited battles with different leads.
3. Play the official daily and share your grid.
4. On slow days, replay an archive team you lost.

Strategy reference: [how to beat Red](https://daily-red.app/how-to-beat-red)
