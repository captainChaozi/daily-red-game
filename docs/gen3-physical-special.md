# Gen III physical / special split used by Daily Red

[Daily Red](https://daily-red.app/) battles use Gen III Showdown rules, where the category of a damaging move is fixed by its type.

| Category | Types |
|---|---|
| Physical | Normal, Fighting, Flying, Poison, Ground, Rock, Bug, Ghost, Steel |
| Special | Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Psychic, Ice, Dragon, Dark |

## Red's damaging moves by category

| Pokémon | Physical | Special |
|---|---|---|
| Pikachu (Light Ball) | Quick Attack, Brick Break | Thunderbolt |
| Charizard (Lum Berry) | Earthquake, Aerial Ace | Flamethrower, Dragon Claw |
| Blastoise (Leftovers) | – | Surf, Ice Beam |
| Venusaur (Leftovers) | – | Giga Drain |
| Snorlax (Leftovers) | Body Slam, Shadow Ball, Earthquake | – |
| Espeon (Lum Berry) | – | Psychic |

Status moves (Thunder Wave, Toxic, Protect, Sleep Powder, Leech Seed, Rest, Calm Mind, Reflect, Morning Sun) are not listed.

## Practical reading

- Snorlax is an all-physical attacker here, Shadow Ball included.
- Charizard is mixed, so one wall rarely covers all four moves.
- Espeon's Reflect halves physical damage to its side, which hurts physical attackers you bring.
