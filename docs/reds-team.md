# Red's team and matchup notes

Reference for [Daily Red Pokemon](https://daily-red.app/) (REDLINE), a free daily Gen III Showdown battle against a fixed Red. Your six change every day at 00:00 UTC.

## Red's six (all level 100)
| Pokémon | Ability / item | Moves |
|---|---|---|
| Pikachu (always leads) | Static / Light Ball | Thunderbolt, Quick Attack, Thunder Wave, Brick Break |
| Charizard | Blaze / Lum Berry | Flamethrower, Dragon Claw, Earthquake, Aerial Ace |
| Blastoise | Torrent / Leftovers | Surf, Ice Beam, Toxic, Protect |
| Venusaur | Overgrow / Leftovers | Giga Drain, Sleep Powder, Leech Seed, Protect |
| Snorlax | Thick Fat / Leftovers | Body Slam, Shadow Ball, Earthquake, Rest |
| Espeon | Synchronize / Lum Berry | Psychic, Calm Mind, Reflect, Morning Sun |

## Gen III category rule
Physical or special is decided by the move's type. Shadow Ball (Ghost) is physical; Dragon Claw (Dragon) is special.

## Planning checklist
- [ ] Answer to the Pikachu lead (watch Thunder Wave)
- [ ] Who removes Snorlax through Thick Fat and Rest
- [ ] Plan before Espeon sets up Calm Mind / Reflect
- [ ] Coverage for Charizard, Blastoise and Venusaur
